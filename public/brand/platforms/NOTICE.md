# Platform logos — trademark notice

The 115 SVG files in this directory are used **only to identify** the platform a
public profile belongs to. All logos and brand names are trademarks of their
respective owners. Their presence here does not imply partnership, sponsorship
or endorsement.

- Most icons come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) (CC0 1.0) and are
  stored locally so the app makes no third-party image requests.
- A few icons (`craftrise.svg` and some niche platforms) come from the platform's
  own public favicon/manifest asset and are stored locally for identification only.
- The app never hotlinks an external image; CSP allows only `self`, `data:` and
  `blob:` images.
- If you are a rights holder and want an icon removed or updated, open an issue
  in this repository; the icon will be replaced with a neutral monogram.

Coverage includes social networks, forums, blogs, developer platforms, video,
gaming, music, messaging, streaming, professional, education, commerce, photos,
reading and other public sites. The canonical list is
`src/server/search/platforms.ts`; each icon filename matches a platform `id`.
Examples: github, gitlab, codeberg, hackernews, devto, npm, pypi, soundcloud,
youtube, steam, lichess, chesscom, roblox, reddit, instagram, x, tiktok, twitch,
pinterest, spotify, discord, telegram, facebook, snapchat, netflix, medium,
craftrise, vimeo, behance, dribbble, substack, patreon, huggingface, kaggle,
lastfm, flickr, goodreads, myanimelist, kick, rumble, vk, okru, uludagsozluk,
technopat, r10, sahibinden, trendyol, eksisozluk.
