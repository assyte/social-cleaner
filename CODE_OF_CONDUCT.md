# Code of Conduct

Social Cleaner is a privacy and identity-safety project. Everyone participating in the
repository, issues, discussions, and pull requests is expected to act respectfully and to
protect other people's data.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and constructive. Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Assume good faith, ask clarifying questions, and explain tradeoffs.
- Use synthetic data in examples, tests, and screenshots.
- Respect privacy: do not post another person's identity, contact details, profile links,
  or search results.
- Follow the ownership rule: do not use the project to investigate someone else's identity.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language.
- Doxxing, stalking, or publishing private or identifying information.
- Requests or contributions that enable third-party surveillance, account takeover, or
  bypassing platform access controls.
- Deliberately adding false coverage claims, hidden tracking, or deceptive payment flows.
- Publishing a security report or exploit before a fix is available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when these rules are violated. Reports can be sent privately to
[@assyte](https://github.com/assyte) on GitHub. Do not include third-party personal data in
a report.

This code applies to project spaces and to public interactions made on the project's behalf.
